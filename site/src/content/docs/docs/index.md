Brahma is the governed local AI org: a company of AI agents on your Mac. Agents propose work, the engine verifies and lands it, and you approve what only you can decide. These pages are for the person who runs it.

- [Quick start](/start/) — from source to a running company in five steps.
- [Concepts](/docs/concepts/) — companies, agents and roles, work items, asks, sprints, modes and pacing, reviews and the merge queue, memory, the handbook, resources, previews and the agent map.
- [Guides](/docs/guides/) — short tasks: add an agent, set a main check, drop files for agents, purge a resource, deploy a preview, run from the terminal, back up and restore.
- [Safety](/docs/safety/) — profiles, the sandbox, taint and vouch, secrets, the canary, the emergency stop and backups: what each piece does and what it does not.
- [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) — symptoms and their exact checks and fixes.
- [CLI reference](/docs/cli/) — every `brahma` command and flag, generated from this build's help.

How Brahma is built, module by module, is under [Inside Brahma](/internals/).
