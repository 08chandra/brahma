## Purpose

The Mac app's state, with no AppKit or SwiftUI import. `AppModel` holds a `Store` and polls it on a tick into an immutable `Snapshot` the views render; `WorkSnapshot`, `PreviewSnapshot`, `ReleasesSnapshot` and `HandbookSnapshot` are its parts. `EngineHost` runs the orchestrator inside the app under the per-home `OrchestratorLock`. `MapController` feeds one company's map: snapshots in, traffic in, animations out. `LaunchOptions` parses the app's command-line flags. Presentation types turn store rows into strings and rows; the forms validate owner input. Everything here is testable without a window.

## Key types

- [`AppModel`](/api/brahmaappmodel/documentation/brahmaappmodel/appmodel/) — the one observable root: the store, the selection, the open sheet and the sub-models.
- [`Snapshot`](/api/brahmaappmodel/documentation/brahmaappmodel/snapshot/) — what the views render, loaded off the main actor on each tick.
- [`EngineHost`](/api/brahmaappmodel/documentation/brahmaappmodel/enginehost/) — the orchestrator in-process; its `EngineStatus` is `starting`, `embedded`, `external`, `stopping`, `stopped` or `failed`.
- [`MapController`](/api/brahmaappmodel/documentation/brahmaappmodel/mapcontroller/) — one company's map: `sync`, `poll` and `frame`.
- [`LaunchOptions`](/api/brahmaappmodel/documentation/brahmaappmodel/launchoptions/) and [`SelectTarget`](/api/brahmaappmodel/documentation/brahmaappmodel/selecttarget/) — the app's command-line flags and what `--select` can open.
- [`NotificationPosting`](/api/brahmaappmodel/documentation/brahmaappmodel/notificationposting/) — the protocol the app's banner poster implements.

## How it connects

- **Calls BrahmaCore.** `AppModel` owns a `Store` and loads `Snapshot.load(store:)` off the main actor; `EngineHost` acquires the `OrchestratorLock` and builds an `Orchestrator`; a live `MapController` reads a `Store` through `MapSource.live`; `OwnerKeyRotation.rotate` calls `OwnerKey.create` with an `OwnerKeyStore`.
- **Called by BrahmaApp.** `AppDelegate` parses `LaunchOptions`, builds the `AppModel` and the `EngineHost`; the views read `model.snapshot`; the menu bar renders `MenuBarSummary`; `UNNotificationPoster` is the app's `NotificationPosting`.
- **Called by BrahmaMapKit.** `OrgScene` plays what `MapController.frame(now:)` returns and lays out with `OrgLayout.make`; `MapPalette` builds its colours from `ThemeHex`; every label is `MapText.clean` output.
- **BrahmaKeychain.** `Package.swift` lists it as a dependency, but no file in this target imports it: the Keychain reaches the model only as the `SecretVault` and `OwnerKeyStore` values the app passes to `AppModel` and `EngineHost`.
