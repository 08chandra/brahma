## Purpose

`MessageRouter` is the one way a message enters the store: it writes the row, fans out inbox items to the recipients, and marks which of them wake. `WakePolicy` is the rule: channel membership is not attention. Only a direct address (a DM or an `@mention`), a reply to someone the agent waits on, or a permitted `@here` or `@team` broadcast wakes an agent; broadcasts are limited to managers and executives and rate-limited per hour. System messages never wake anybody. A message whose text impersonates the owner is flagged on the way in.

## Key types

- [`MessageRouter`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/messagerouter/) — `post` and `sendDM`.
- [`WakePolicy`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/wakepolicy/) — who a message wakes, and the broadcast limits.
- [`PostResult`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/postresult/) — what a post did, including any refusal text.
- [`NoticeRecipientRole`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/noticerecipientrole/) — how a recipient relates to the item an event is about.
- [`RouterError`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/routererror/) — a refused post.

## How it connects

- **Called by Store.** The ask lifecycle, asks and the budget step send their system DMs through `MessageRouter(store:)`.
- **Called by MCP.** `CompanyTools` posts agents' messages through the router.
- **Called by Orchestrator, Engine and Work.** Timer-fired turns, engine alerts and the watchdog's DMs all use it.
- **Calls Safety.** `OwnerImpersonation.flags` marks text that pretends to be the owner.
