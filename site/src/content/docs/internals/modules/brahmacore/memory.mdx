## Purpose

`MemoryIndex` is one scoped full-text index (FTS5) over everything a company has written: messages, items, asks, decisions, hand-offs, retros, handbook pages, resources and remembered facts. Every query is bound to one company and read through the access engine, so a doc of another company answers exactly like a missing one. `MemoryIngest` is the tick step that advances a cursor per source and upserts new docs; its cursors survive a restore. `EpisodeRecorder` writes an `episode` entry at the end of each successful turn, never through a model. `MemoryQuery` turns typed text into quoted FTS literals only. `Replay` renders one agent's day or one item's history; `StorageMeter` measures a company's footprint.

## Key types

- [`MemoryIndex`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/memoryindex/) — search and read by ref, filtered by access.
- [`MemoryIngest`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/memoryingest/) and [`MemoryIngestDoc`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/memoryingestdoc/) — the tick step and one doc to index.
- [`ReaderContext`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/readercontext/) and [`AccessClass`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/accessclass/) — who is reading, and who a doc belongs to.
- [`MemoryEntry`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/memoryentry/) — one remembered fact or recorded episode.
- [`EpisodeRecorder`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/episoderecorder/) — turn-end episodes.
- [`Replay`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/replay/) — a day or an item as a list of lines.

## How it connects

- **Called by Orchestrator.** Each tick runs `MemoryIngest.run` per company; the restore hook resets its cursors; the turn-end step records an episode.
- **Called by MCP.** `search_memory` and `recall` go through `MemoryIndex`.
- **Called by Store and Resources.** `Store+Memory` holds `ingest`; owner resources are indexed as `MemoryIngestDoc`s.
- **Called by Handbook.** `KBAccess` loads the reader's `ReaderContext` here.
- **Read by the app.** The storage panel calls `StorageMeter.measure`.
