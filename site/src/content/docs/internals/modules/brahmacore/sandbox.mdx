## Purpose

The sandbox is the structural wall between an agent's turn and the owner's data, the engine's database and files, the common git dir and the other agents. `SandboxScope.forTurn` computes what one turn may read and write. `ClaudeSettingsCompiler` turns that scope, the profile and the mode into the settings file every turn runs with (`--settings <file>`, `--setting-sources ""`), including the permission deny rules for the high-risk company tools. `ShellPrefix` is the Bash prefix. For commands the engine runs itself, `SeatbeltRunner` runs `sandbox-exec -p <profile> /bin/sh -c <command>` with a `SeatbeltProfile` built from the same scope, and `EgressProxy` is an HTTP CONNECT proxy on loopback that admits TLS to allowed domains on port 443 only. Off macOS, `UnavailableSandboxRunner` fails closed.

## Key types

- [`SandboxScope`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/sandboxscope/) and [`GrantInputs`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/grantinputs/) — what a turn may touch, and the grants that widen it.
- [`ClaudeSettings`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/claudesettings/) and [`ClaudeSettingsCompiler`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/claudesettingscompiler/) — the settings file and its compiler.
- [`SandboxedRunner`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/sandboxedrunner/) and [`SeatbeltRunner`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/seatbeltrunner/) — the protocol and its macOS implementation.
- [`SeatbeltProfile`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/seatbeltprofile/) — the SBPL rules of one command.
- [`EgressProxy`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/egressproxy/) — the loopback CONNECT proxy.
- [`SandboxedCheckRunner`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/sandboxedcheckrunner/) — checks under the sandbox.

## How it connects

- **Called by Orchestrator.** Before a turn, `Orchestrator+Sandbox` builds the `SandboxLayout`, writes the `ShellPrefix` and computes `SandboxScope.forTurn`.
- **Called by Turns.** `TurnProfileCompiler` calls `ClaudeSettingsCompiler.compile`, and a `ClaudeCommand` carries its `SandboxScope`.
- **Called by Preview, Extensions and Release.** Preview adapters run under a `SandboxPolicy`; the extension importer unzips under the `SandboxedRunner`; the smoke check on `main` reclassifies nested-sandbox failures through `SandboxedCheckRunner`.
- **Calls Verify.** `SandboxedCheckRunner` implements the verify area's `CheckRunning` and falls back to `ProcessCheckRunner`.
- **Calls MCP and Safety.** The settings deny `CompanyTools.highRiskTools`; the Seatbelt runner records its child in `ChildGroups`.
