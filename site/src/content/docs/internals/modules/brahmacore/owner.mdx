## Purpose

An `OwnerNotice` is an engine message to the owner that is not an ask. `OwnerPrefs` holds the owner's notification, quiet-hours, digest and sleep settings. `DigestBuilder` renders the daily digest with agent text escaped, never markup. `HumanTask` templates are the tasks the owner does by hand (an account, a domain, test keys), with engine-written steps, links and paste-back fields. `NoticeDelivery` plans which banners and pushes go out; `PushDispatcher` sends them through an ntfy or Pushover channel, coalesced outside quiet hours, capped per day, marked pushed only after a 2xx. `AskQuota` caps the open owner requests one agent may hold.

## Key types

- [`OwnerNotice`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/ownernotice/) and [`OwnerPrefs`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/ownerprefs/) — a notice, and the owner's settings.
- [`DigestBuilder`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/digestbuilder/) — the daily digest.
- [`HumanTask`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/humantask/) and [`HumanTaskTemplate`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/humantasktemplate/) — tasks for the owner's hands.
- [`NoticeDelivery`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/noticedelivery/) — the delivery plan.
- [`PushDispatcher`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/pushdispatcher/) and [`PushChannel`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/pushchannel/) — sending phone pushes.
- [`AskQuota`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/askquota/) — the per-agent ask limit.

## How it connects

- **Called by Orchestrator.** The engine constructs the `PushDispatcher` and ticks it as a detached task after the timers step.
- **Called by Store.** `Store+Digest` builds the digest through `DigestBuilder.build`; `Store+HumanTasks` files templates from `HumanTasks`; ask creation takes an `AskQuota.Exemption`.
- **Calls Safety.** A push channel reads the owner's credential from the `SecretVault`, never from the database.
- **Calls Timers.** Quiet hours and the digest time are computed with `TimerSchedule.daily`.
