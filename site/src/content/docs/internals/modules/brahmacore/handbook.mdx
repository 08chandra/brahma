## Purpose

The handbook is the company's own documentation: pages named by a normalised path, each a chain of append-only revisions, split into sections by heading. `KBAccess` decides who may read which section, through the same access engine company memory uses, so a restriction marker on a heading caps everything under it. `AutoPages` are the pages the engine renders from live data and nobody else writes. `HandbookExporter` mirrors the handbook into the workspace as Markdown files. `HandbookPath` normalises page names so the same name typed two ways is one page.

## Key types

- [`KBPage`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/kbpage/), [`KBRevision`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/kbrevision/) and [`KBSection`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/kbsection/) — a page, one revision, one section.
- [`KBAccess`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/kbaccess/) — who may read a page or a section.
- [`KBView`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/kbview/) — a page as one reader may see it.
- [`AutoPages`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/autopages/) and [`HandbookRefresh`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/handbookrefresh/) — the engine-rendered pages and when they refresh.
- [`HandbookExporter`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/handbookexporter/) — the Markdown mirror.
- [`HandbookPath`](/api/brahmacore/documentation/brahmacore/handbookpath/) — a normalised page name.

## How it connects

- **Called by Store.** `Store+Handbook` filters every read and backlink through `KBAccess.canReadPage` and `KBAccess.canRead`.
- **Called by MCP.** `search_kb`, `kb_read` and `kb_write` call `store.kbRead` and `store.kbWrite`.
- **Called by Orchestrator.** Each tick, `HandbookRefresh.run` re-renders the auto pages of companies whose interval is due.
- **Called by Backup.** A company export writes the mirror through `HandbookExporter.export`.
- **Calls Memory.** `KBAccess` loads the reader's `ReaderContext`; `AutoPages` reads taint through `MemoryIngest`.
